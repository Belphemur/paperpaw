The reader shows books page by page. Open a book from the library; the last book can reopen at start (setting Open last book at start).

Tap the left or right of the screen, or use the page buttons, to turn pages. Tap the middle or press Select for the reading menu: Contents, Search, Go to page, Font and layout, Bookmarks and highlights and more. Down from the top edge opens the quick panel, up from the bottom edge goes home; so does holding Back.

Where the device has a front light, a slide along its sides sets it: along the left edge for its warmth, along the right one for its brightness (sliding up for both to grow). Only the light changes, no page is drawn again; a swipe across in a band still turns the page, and the outermost strip at each side stays the page turns' and the quick panel's. The bands' width is a setting, *Light bands at the sides*; at 0 they are off.

Tap the top right corner for a bookmark. Hold a word to highlight it; with buttons, use Select text under More; Left and Right then move by word, the page buttons by line. Export as Markdown saves your notes.
