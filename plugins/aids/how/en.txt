Reading aids change how the text of a book looks while you read. They apply to every book. Bold word beginnings and Replace words are off at first.

You find them in Settings, under Reading, in the section Reading aids. Bold word beginnings sets the first half of each word in bold, which some people find easier to follow. Replace words takes pairs written as "colour = color; Mr. = Mister", separated by semicolons.

Only whole words are replaced, exactly as you wrote them, so "color" never changes part of a longer word. The book file itself is not changed.

Pictures in books can be shown (as at first), turned into a short line such as "[Picture: a ship at sea]" that uses the picture's description, or left out. A page then has more room for text, and nothing is decoded for a picture that is not shown, which makes pages turn faster. Comics and other books made of pictures are not changed.
